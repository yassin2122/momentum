# Momentum

> **Level Up Your Body. Master Real Skills. Train Together.**

Momentum is a premium fitness platform that combines structured strength training, skill progression, and social motivation into a single experience.

Unlike traditional workout trackers that only record sets and reps, Momentum turns fitness into a long-term journey with goals, unlocks, achievements, and a community that grows with you.

---

# Vision

Fitness shouldn't feel like checking boxes.

It should feel like progressing through a game.

Every workout should move you closer to unlocking a new ability, reaching a new milestone, or becoming stronger than yesterday.

Momentum transforms real fitness progress into a rewarding journey while remaining grounded in science-based training.

---

# Pillar 2 — Progress

## Vision

Momentum transforms years of calisthenics practice into a structured, coach-approved progression system.

Every unlocked skill represents a genuine athletic achievement—not something earned by grinding XP.

The experience should feel like:

* Apple Fitness quality
* Duolingo progression
* Professional calisthenics coaching

No fantasy powers.

No unrealistic unlocks.

Every milestone reflects real physical ability.

---

## Fitness Universe

The progression system is organized into **Galaxies**.

A Galaxy represents a major training discipline.

Each Galaxy contains multiple **Planets**.

Each Planet represents one advanced skill.

Inside every Planet is the complete progression roadmap required to master that skill.

```
Fitness Universe

├── Pull Galaxy
│   ├── Muscle-Up Planet
│   ├── Front Lever Planet
│   ├── Back Lever Planet
│   ├── Human Flag Planet
│   └── One-Arm Pull-Up Planet
│
├── Push Galaxy
│   ├── Handstand Planet
│   ├── Handstand Push-Up Planet
│   ├── One-Arm Handstand Planet
│   └── Planche Planet
│
├── Core Galaxy
│   ├── L-Sit Planet
│   ├── V-Sit Planet
│   └── Dragon Flag Planet
│
└── Legs Galaxy
    └── Pistol Squat Planet
```

Each Galaxy is a clean constellation of interconnected planets, making long-term progression easy to understand while preserving a premium aesthetic.

## Skill Requirements

Every roadmap node contains measurable objectives.

Examples:

* Hold duration
* Number of repetitions
* Technique checkpoints
* Consistency goals

---

## XP

XP is **visual feedback only**.

It reflects mastery within the current roadmap.

XP never unlocks a skill by itself.

Example:

```
High Pull-Up

Progress

████████░░

82%

680 / 800 XP
```

Even at 100% XP, the user must still complete every objective before advancing.

---

## Objective-Based Progression

A node unlocks only after all required objectives are completed.

Example:

High Pull-Up

Requirements

✓ 12 explosive pull-ups

✓ Pull to lower chest

✓ 5 controlled repetitions

✓ Two successful training sessions

Estimated time

2–4 weeks

Progress

82%

Only when every objective is satisfied does the next node become available.

---

## Completion Levels

Every Planet has three certifications.

### Complete

Successfully perform the skill once.

Example: One clean Muscle-Up.

---

### Master

Perform the skill consistently with reliable technique.

Example: Five consecutive clean Muscle-Ups.

---

### Legendary

Elite-level performance under strict form standards.

Example:

Perfect bar path.

No kipping.

Controlled tempo.

Multiple flawless repetitions.

Legendary certification represents years of dedicated training and should be highly respected within the community.

---

# Pillar 3 — Connect

## Purpose

Fitness is better with real expert guidance, not a crowd of strangers cheering from a feed.

Connect is how a user finds and trains under a real coach — a **Captain**.

There is no public feed. No posts, likes, or comments. No friends list. No leaderboards. Connect is private, 1:1, and built entirely around real training data — not social performance.

---

## Finding a Captain

* Browse the coach directory — specialty, bio, certifications, capacity
* Request a Captain
* The coach accepts or declines

Only one active Captain per athlete at a time.

---

## The Captain Relationship

* The athlete decides exactly what the Captain can see — workouts, PRs, skill progress, body metrics — each shared independently and revocable at any time
* Ending the relationship removes the Captain's access immediately
* Everything the Captain sees is real, unedited training history — never a summary the athlete curated for show

---

## What a Captain Can Do

* View whatever the athlete has permitted, nothing else
* Set goals for the athlete
* Assign one of their own training programs
* Leave feedback on a specific workout or skill
* Keep private coaching notes the athlete never sees

---

## What a Captain Cannot Do

* See anything outside the athlete's granted permissions
* Edit or delete any athlete log, PR, or progress record — a Captain's access is read-only, so training history always stays trustworthy
* Charge or process payment inside the app (plan pricing is informational only, for now)

---

## Design Goal

Connect should feel like hiring a real coach, not joining a social network — a quiet, trustworthy line between one athlete and one Captain, built on real data instead of public performance.
