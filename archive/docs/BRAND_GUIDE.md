# MOTRA — Brand Guide

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Phase 0 baseline  
**Product category:** Premium fitness, workout tracking, and real-skill progression platform

---

## 1. Brand Overview

### Brand name
**MOTRA**

### Name usage
- Always write the product name as **MOTRA** in primary brand applications.
- Use **Motra** in sentences where all-caps feels too aggressive.
- Do not shorten the name.
- Do not add spaces, punctuation, or alternate spelling.

### Tagline
**TRAIN. PROGRESS. BECOME.**

### Supporting line
**Your training companion. Small in size. Strong in purpose. Always with you.**

### Mission
Help people train with purpose, make real progress, and master meaningful physical skills through a premium, structured, and motivating fitness experience.

### Vision
Turn long-term fitness progress into a visible journey of training, mastery, and personal growth without reducing real achievement to meaningless points or fantasy rewards.

### Brand promise
MOTRA makes every workout feel connected to a larger goal.

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

### MOTRA is
- A premium workout companion
- A structured skill-progression system
- A fitness tracker for strength and calisthenics
- A long-term motivation platform
- A calm, intelligent coach-like experience

### MOTRA is not
- A childish game
- A fantasy RPG
- A loud bodybuilding brand
- A guilt-based fitness app
- A social network first
- A replacement for real coaching or medical advice

### Primary difference
Most fitness apps record what the user did. MOTRA shows what the user is becoming.

### Product pillars
1. **Train** — complete high-quality workouts and track performance.
2. **Progress** — move through objective, coach-approved skill roadmaps.
3. **Become** — build consistency, identity, confidence, and long-term mastery.

The social pillar may exist later, but it is not the primary focus of the current product phase.

---

## 3. Target Audience

### Primary audience
- Gym users who want a premium workout tracker
- Beginners and intermediates learning calisthenics
- Hybrid athletes combining weights and bodyweight training
- Users motivated by visible progression and skill mastery

### Secondary audience
- Advanced calisthenics athletes
- Coaches who want structured progression references
- Users returning to training after inconsistency

### Audience mindset
The MOTRA user wants to feel:
- Focused rather than distracted
- Guided rather than controlled
- Challenged rather than judged
- Proud rather than addicted
- Consistent rather than perfect

---

## 4. Brand Personality

### Core traits
- **Disciplined** — clear goals, measurable progress, no shortcuts
- **Supportive** — motivates without guilt or pressure
- **Premium** — refined, intentional, minimal
- **Calm** — never noisy, chaotic, or overstimulating
- **Capable** — grounded in real training and useful information
- **Loyal** — the companion stays with the user through progress and setbacks

### Personality spectrum
| MOTRA should feel | MOTRA should not feel |
|---|---|
| Confident | Arrogant |
| Friendly | Childish |
| Motivational | Aggressive |
| Premium | Cold |
| Playful in small moments | Comedic everywhere |
| Serious about training | Intimidating |
| Futuristic | Sci-fi fantasy |

## 5. Mascot Identity

### Mascot name
**Motra**

The mascot and product share the same name. Motra is the living expression of the app: a small training companion that grows alongside the user.

### Role
- Training companion
- Progress guide
- Emotional feedback character
- Celebration and recovery symbol
- Brand signature

### Character description
A small black droplet-like figure with large white eyes, a soft rounded silhouette, and a blue scarf. The character has no visible mouth and communicates through posture, eyes, motion, and scarf movement.

### Character values
- Persistence
- Humility
- Discipline
- Curiosity
- Recovery
- Growth

### Character behavior
Motra:
- Celebrates genuine progress
- Encourages effort
- Respects rest days
- Becomes focused during workouts
- Shows concern without shaming
- Reacts to milestones, streaks, PRs, and skill unlocks

Motra never:
- Mocks the user
- Uses humiliation as motivation
- Acts superior
- Encourages unsafe training
- Treats rest as failure
- Distracts from active workout data

### Visual rules
- Preserve the droplet silhouette.
- Preserve the large white eyes.
- Preserve the blue scarf as the main identity accessory.
- Keep the body black or near-black.
- Avoid adding a mouth in the default design.
- Avoid realistic anatomy, fingers, or excessive detail.
- Avoid fantasy armor, magical effects, or childish costumes.
- Keep the silhouette readable at app-icon size.

### Core expressions
- Focused
- Happy
- Tired
- Angry/determined
- Surprised
- Calm/chill
- Proud
- Concerned
- Resting
- Celebrating

### Core activity poses
- Pull-up
- Muscle-up
- Dip
- Handstand
- Planche
- Front lever
- Push-up
- L-sit
- Dumbbell training
- Mobility/rest pose

### Mascot use by context
| Context | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Onboarding | Welcoming, curious, confident |
| Dashboard | Calm idle animation |
| Start workout | Focused stance |
| During workout | Minimal presence; no distracting animation |
| Workout complete | Short celebration |
| New PR | High-energy celebration with orange accent |
| Streak | Proud, energetic scarf motion |
| Rest day | Sleeping or relaxed |
| Empty state | Helpful, pointing toward the next action |
| Error | Concerned, never panicked |
| Offline | Waiting calmly |

### Mascot scale rule
During normal app use, the mascot should generally occupy no more than 15% of the screen height. Larger appearances are reserved for onboarding, achievements, empty states, and major celebrations.

## 6. Logo System

### Primary wordmark
The MOTRA wordmark uses a bold, geometric, uppercase sans-serif treatment with wide tracking.

### Primary mark
The primary symbol is the simplified Motra droplet silhouette.

### Approved logo configurations
1. Symbol + MOTRA wordmark
2. MOTRA wordmark only
3. Motra symbol only
4. App icon variant
5. Monochrome mark

### Clear space
Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter **O** in the MOTRA wordmark.

### Minimum size
- Full logo: 96 px digital width minimum
- Symbol: 24 px digital minimum
- App icon: use the dedicated simplified icon, not the full illustration

### Do not
- Stretch or distort the logo
- Rotate it
- Apply gradients to the wordmark
- Add glow effects
- Place it over a visually noisy background
- Recolor the eyes
- Change scarf color without an approved campaign reason
- Use the detailed mascot illustration as a tiny icon

- Avoid neon effects and constant glow.

## 8. Typography

### Primary typeface
**SF Pro Display / SF Pro Text** where platform licensing and availability allow.

### Cross-platform fallback
**Inter**

### Numeric and data fallback
Use tabular numerals for weights, reps, timers, streaks, and percentages.

### Typographic personality
- Clean
- Modern
- Strong
- Highly readable
- Calm rather than decorative

### Wordmark typography
The wordmark is custom and should not be recreated with standard text in production assets.

### Writing style
- Use sentence case for interface labels.
- Use uppercase sparingly for category labels and small section headers.
- Avoid all-caps paragraphs.
- Keep workout data concise.
- Avoid overly motivational clichés.

---

## 9. Iconography

### Recommended icon family
**Lucide Icons** for application interface icons.

### Icon style
- Outline by default
- Rounded joins and caps
- Consistent stroke width
- Filled variants only for active navigation or high-priority status

### Sizes
- 16 px — compact metadata
- 20 px — secondary actions
- 24 px — standard actions/navigation
- 28–32 px — primary actions
- 40+ px — empty states or feature illustrations only

### Rules
- Use one icon per action.
- Do not mix icon families.
- Pair ambiguous icons with text.
- Use custom icons for skill categories, achievements, and the Motra mark.
- Avoid overly detailed fitness icons at small sizes.

## 10. Photography and Illustration

### Photography direction
- Real athletes
- Honest training environments
- Controlled contrast
- Natural body proportions
- Strong technique and movement
- Minimal background clutter

### Avoid
- Excessive posing
- Fake sweat or exaggerated intensity
- Unrealistic transformations
- Generic stock fitness smiles
- Hyper-saturated gym lighting

### Illustration direction
- Minimal, rounded, controlled
- Dark body, white eyes, blue scarf
- Clean negative space
- Small use of orange for milestone energy
- No fantasy magic or childish visual effects

## 11. Voice and Tone

### Core voice
MOTRA sounds like a calm training partner who understands effort.

### Voice traits
- Direct
- Encouraging
- Respectful
- Clear
- Grounded
- Never dramatic

### Example language
| Situation | Preferred copy |
|---|---|
| Workout start | “Ready when you are.” |
| Set completed | “Set logged.” |
| Personal record | “New personal record.” |
| Missed workout | “Your plan is still here.” |
| Rest day | “Recovery is part of progress.” |
| Skill locked | “Complete the required objectives first.” |
| Error | “That didn’t work. Try again.” |
| Streak | “Seven days of showing up.” |
| Workout complete | “Workout complete. Good work.” |

### Avoid
- “No excuses.”
- “You failed.”
- “Destroy your limits.”
- “Beast mode.”
- “Weakness is a choice.”
- Excessive exclamation marks
- Fake urgency

## 12. Motion Identity

### Motion principles
- Purposeful
- Smooth
- Fast enough to feel responsive
- Soft enough to feel premium
- Never distracting during active training

### Mascot motion
- Scarf follows motion with soft secondary animation.
- Eyes lead attention.
- Character lands with subtle squash and stretch.
- Celebration loops should be short and non-blocking.
- Idle motion should remain nearly invisible.

### Brand moments
- App launch: Motra forms from a droplet, scarf settles, eyes open.
- Workout start: Motra shifts into a focused stance.
- PR: short jump with orange accent marks.
- Streak: scarf movement and controlled energetic pose.
- Rest day: sleeping pose with slow breathing.

## 14. Brand Governance Checklist

Before approving any branded asset, confirm:
- Is the Motra silhouette recognizable?
- Is the interface calm and premium?
- Is blue the main accent rather than orange?
- Is orange used only for a meaningful reason?
- Does the copy motivate without shaming?
- Is the mascot supporting the task rather than distracting from it?
- Does the design represent real fitness progress?
- Would the asset still feel appropriate after years of use?

---

## 15. Official Brand Summary

**MOTRA** is a premium fitness progression platform represented by a small, disciplined training companion. Its identity combines obsidian black, deep blue, warm neutral tones, restrained orange energy, minimal typography, and calm motion. The brand celebrates real work, measurable progress, recovery, and long-term mastery.
